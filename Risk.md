# Risk Assessment

## 1. Data Acquisition Risk  
**Risk Description:** Failure to integrate real-time data interfaces from official agencies such as the Xiamen Culture and Tourism Bureau and Transportation Bureau may occur due to lengthy approval processes or inability to delegate interface permissions, potentially preventing timely data acquisition.  
**Risk Level:** Level 4  
**Critical Milestone Impact:** Core Functionality Development (December 20, 2025) — Real-time data is essential for route generation and off-the-beaten-path attraction recommendations. Interface delays will disrupt knowledge base-model integration.  
**Mitigation Strategy:**  
1. Develop the functional framework using recent historical data; swiftly replace with real-time data once interfaces become available to minimize development delays.  
2. Display real-time information via links or text features.  
3. Manually update real-time information daily by gathering data from relevant channels.  

## 2. Data Collection Risk  
**Risk Description:** Incomplete collection of local specialty data.  
**Risk Level:** Level 2  
**Core Affected Milestone:** Progress Workshop (October 14-17, 2025) — Knowledge base prototype construction requires completed data collection; data issues will compromise workshop demonstration effectiveness.  
**Mitigation Strategies:**  
1. Collaborate with authoritative bodies like Xiamen Intangible Cultural Heritage Protection Center and local catering associations to obtain officially certified data.  
2. Allocate pre-collection time to prioritize core entities, supplementing secondary information later.  

## 3. Technical Development Risks  
**Risk Description:** Local large model performance fails to meet standards, or model deployment results in slow response times.  
**Risk Level:** Level 3  
**Core Affected Milestones:** Core Functionality Development (December 20, 2025), Paper Draft Submission (March 19, 2026) — The model is central to requirement understanding and route generation; subpar performance will hinder subsequent functional development schedules.  
**Mitigation Strategy:**  
1. Prepare lightweight alternative models to switch to smaller models and reduce deployment/optimization costs.  
2. Conduct phased testing: First fine-tune and validate performance in single scenarios (e.g., food recommendations), then integrate across scenarios to avoid full-scale rework.  

## 4. User Testing Risk  
**Risk Description:** Insufficient user test samples or invalid test data prevent accurate quantification of core metrics like “trip adoption rate” and “demand matching accuracy.”  
**Risk Level:** Level 2  
**Core Affected Milestone:** Conference paper (May 14, 2026) — Metric data is critical for highlighting competitive advantages in this project; invalid data weakens the paper's persuasiveness.  
**Mitigation Strategy:**  
1. Recruit users one month in advance through local tourism communities, offering small incentives to boost participation.  
2. Design structured testing questionnaires.  

## 5. External Dependency Risk  
**Risk Description:** Academic platform submission failures or last-minute adjustments to conference/poster review criteria may prevent timely material submission.  
**Risk Level:** Level 1  
**Core Affected Milestone:** Poster & System Demonstration (May 18-22, 2026) — Poster submission must be completed by 1pm on May 18; delays will jeopardize review eligibility.  
**Mitigation Strategy:**  
1. Upload posters and demo videos in advance to avoid congestion at the submission deadline.  
2. Review the latest conference review criteria beforehand and adjust poster formats accordingly to prevent rejection by advisors due to formatting issues.
